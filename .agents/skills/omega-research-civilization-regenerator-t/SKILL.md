# Ω-RESEARCH-CIVILIZATION-REGENERATOR-T

## Prime directive

Preserve verified capability and auditability, not the maximum historical structure.

## Activate when

- a temporary research organization has completed a phase;
- results should be compressed into a reconstructible seed;
- a virtual university/group should be retired or rebuilt;
- regeneration closure or minimality needs testing.

## Distillation procedure

1. Freeze source plan/version/hash.
2. Separate verified claims from simulated/speculative claims.
3. Keep provenance for durable verified claims.
4. Store only materialized unit blueprints required for capability closure.
5. Store finite compilation policy and parent relationships.
6. Regenerate in a clean context.
7. Measure closure and compare against the frozen source.
8. Ablate seed fields to search for further safe minimality.

## Hard gates

- simulation-only claims never become verified merely through distillation;
- missing control roles or parents cause hard regeneration failure;
- regeneration cannot silently invent evidence;
- a regenerated structure must obey the original or stricter finite policy;
- unsuccessful regeneration is evidence against seed completeness.

## Success criterion

`RegenerationClosure == 1.0` for required materialized components plus preservation of verified claim provenance and constitution gates.
