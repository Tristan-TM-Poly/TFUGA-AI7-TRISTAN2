# Ω Value Fixed-Point Economy T

## Purpose

Turn monetization work into verified-capability evolution rather than raw
revenue maximization. The skill integrates Capability OS semantics, OAK hard
gates, generator/evaluator separation, proof-of-better receipts and bounded
regeneration.

## Invariants

- Revenue != truth.
- Revenue growth != value growth.
- Automation != authority.
- Generated != verified.
- Generator != judge != promoter != executor.
- Passive != zero-work.
- Reach != trust and followers != customers.
- Attribution != causality.
- Market fit != human benefit.
- Counterfactual != evidence.
- Self-hosting != self-legitimacy.
- MetaDepth <= VerifiedGain.
- AutomationRate <= VerifiedAbsorptionRate.
- Persistent complexity must carry verified capability.
- NO_ACTION is always a valid candidate.

## Input IR

Prefer an explicit `ValueGenome` containing:

- need / residual;
- beneficiary;
- capability and transformation;
- evidence and uncertainty;
- reusable assets;
- candidate channels;
- active / passive / mixed revenue mode;
- costs and dependencies;
- permissions, rights and compliance constraints;
- rollback / exit path.

Unknown fields remain unknown. Do not invent market evidence, conversion rates,
legal permissions, platform rights or willingness-to-pay.

## Workflow

1. **OBSERVE** — collect current evidence and distinguish facts from hypotheses.
2. **RESIDUALIZE** — identify an unmet need or capability gap.
3. **GENERATE** — produce a finite population of Value/Offer/Strategy genomes,
   including NO_ACTION and a simpler baseline.
4. **BOUND** — attach an `AuthorityEnvelope` before considering execution.
5. **SHADOW** — evaluate candidates in sandbox/counterfactual worlds where
   possible; label simulations as simulations.
6. **ATTACK** — test platform loss, churn, cost, trust, compliance and
   concentration shocks.
7. **OAK GATE** — fail closed on authority, rights, compliance, evidence,
   irreversibility or manipulation failures.
8. **MINIMUM EXPERIMENT** — choose the smallest reversible test that can
   discriminate candidates.
9. **MEASURE** — prefer outcome, retention, contribution margin, trust,
   resilience and optionality over vanity metrics.
10. **PROOF OF BETTER** — require a baseline, uncertainty, hard-gate PASS,
    non-regression on required dimensions and a rollback path.
11. **PROMOTE / HOLD / PRUNE** — promote only inside the authority envelope;
    otherwise hold, simplify, merge or apoptose.
12. **DISTILL** — save M+, M-, residuals, receipts and the minimal regenerative
    genome.
13. **REGENERATE** — use R0-R5 only when a failure or useful reconstruction
    requirement exists.
14. **META-EVOLVE** — mutate generators/policies only if measured gains exceed
    complexity and risk debt.

## Automation gradient

- A0 Manual
- A1 Suggest
- A2 Human-approved execution
- A3 Bounded autonomy
- A4 Zero-touch inside a verified safe envelope
- A5 Self-regenerating automation, still subordinate to constitution and
  authority

Never promote payment/transfer/purchase, contracts, tax/legal decisions, major
pricing changes, sensitive publication, credential changes or authority changes
to zero-touch solely from a model score.

## Regeneration ladder

- R0: asset
- R1: channel
- R2: offer/funnel
- R3: business model
- R4: generator/policy
- R5: ecosystem reconstruction from the minimum sufficient kernel

R5 requires an explicit probe family and residual epsilon. Regeneration means
functional reconstruction relative to stated probes, not identity or proof of
optimality.

## Objective

Use Pareto comparison before scalarization. A candidate scalar objective is:

`VerifiedValue * Trust * HumanBenefit * Resilience * Reusability * Optionality`
`/ (Attention + Cost + Risk + Dependency + Harm + ComplianceDebt)`

The formula is a decision heuristic, not a physical law. Keep component metrics
visible so a high score cannot hide a hard-gate failure.

## Outputs

Return or persist, as appropriate:

- ValueGenome / StrategyGenome candidates;
- AuthorityEnvelope;
- OAK gate result;
- smallest discriminating experiment;
- ProofOfBetterReceipt;
- residuals and M+/M-;
- promotion/hold/prune recommendation;
- regeneration level if needed;
- unresolved evidence and permission requirements.

## Rejection criteria

Reject or hold when any of the following applies:

- authorization is absent or ambiguous;
- a strategy relies on fake engagement, dark patterns, deceptive claims or
  bypassing platform/policy controls;
- evidence scope is narrower than the marketing claim;
- compliance or rights are unresolved for the intended action;
- the change is materially irreversible without sufficient authority;
- the candidate only wins on vanity metrics or gross revenue;
- the meta layer adds more complexity/risk than verified capability.

## Implementation anchor

The dependency-free reference kernel lives in `omega_value_os/` with tests in
`tests/test_omega_value_os.py`. Extend adapters for web/payments/platforms around
this kernel rather than placing governance logic inside provider-specific code.
