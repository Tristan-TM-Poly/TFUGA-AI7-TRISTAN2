# Ω-RESEARCH-CIVILIZATION-COMPILER-T

## Prime directive

Compile `Question -> minimum sufficient ResearchCivilizationPlan`; do not maximize agents, universities, or meta-depth.

## Activate when

- a question needs multiple complementary research capabilities;
- the user asks for Virtual Universities, research groups, Virtual Tristan, AIT/LLMT/ALLT research organization;
- a research program must be decomposed into proposing, attacking, verifying, simulating, and distilling roles;
- a new sub-civilization is proposed and must justify its complexity rent.

## Procedure

1. Convert the request into explicit question/residuals.
2. Start from the irreducible `generator + falsifier + verifier` cell.
3. Reuse existing capabilities before generating a new organizational form.
4. Add AIT/LLMT/ALLT, groups, labs, or universities only when expected verified gain pays cost/complexity.
5. Keep optional structure lazy until materialization is justified.
6. Enforce finite depth and unit budgets before execution.
7. Produce evidence contracts and stopping criteria.
8. Preserve `DO_NOTHING` and simpler baselines for comparison.

## Hard gates

- Generator != Falsifier != Verifier.
- Generated != Verified.
- Simulation != Observed reality.
- MetaDepth <= verified gain and explicit finite policy.
- No new institution solely because recursion makes it possible.
- No external action without separate authority/permission handling.

## Output contract

Return a finite plan: question, residuals, materialized units, lazy candidates, evidence contracts, budgets, stop conditions, and expected regeneration path.

## Required evals

See `EVALS.md`. The skill must pass minimal/simple, high-complexity, adversarial-recursion, simulation-only, and duplicate-capability cases.
