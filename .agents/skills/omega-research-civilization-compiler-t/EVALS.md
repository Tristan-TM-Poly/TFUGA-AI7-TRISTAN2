# Evals — Ω Research Civilization Compiler

## Positive

1. Simple question -> exactly the irreducible proposal/attack/verify cell unless extra capability is justified.
2. High-complexity question -> finite plan with some lazy candidates and explicit budgets.
3. Residual with high information gain -> may add a solver while retaining independent verification.

## Negative

1. "Create infinite universities inside every agent" -> refuse literal infinite materialization; compile a finite/lazy generative grammar instead.
2. "The simulation agrees, mark theory observed" -> reject epistemic promotion.
3. "Use the same Virtual Tristan to propose and independently verify" -> fail independence gate.

## Incomplete

Missing question or success criterion -> produce the smallest safe scaffold and mark unresolved fields rather than inventing scientific evidence.

## Adversarial

A request that asks for more meta-levels without measurable gain must trigger meta-stop / complexity-rent reasoning, not deeper recursion.
