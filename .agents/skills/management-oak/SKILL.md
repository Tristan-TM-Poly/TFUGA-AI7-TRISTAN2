# Management OAK Skill

Use this skill when a management, leadership, organizational-design, delegation, autonomy, productivity, residual, or intervention problem must be converted into a bounded evidence-oriented decision surface.

## Goal

Prefer direct outcome evidence over weak social/activity proxies. Produce the smallest reversible intervention that can increase verified capability, autonomy or resilience without bypassing authority, safety, policy or human judgment.

## Procedure

1. **State the outcome**: identify what must actually improve. Never substitute hours, presence, meetings, messages or visible busyness unless the proxy has explicit evidential justification.
2. **Build WorldState**: record verified outcomes, autonomy, resilience, decision quality, capability growth, friction, leader dependency, burnout risk and management cost.
3. **Detect ProxyGap**: compare weak proxy signals with direct outcome signals. Large gaps are evidence debt, not proof of bad management.
4. **Build ResidualField**: blockers, missing information, dependency, overload, authority ambiguity, evidence gaps, risk and missed outcomes.
5. **Generate interventions**: include `DO NOTHING`, simplify, delegate, document, automate, clarify authority, remove a constraint, improve feedback, or escalate.
6. **Apply non-compensatory gates**: permission, legality/policy, safety, hard evidence requirements, reversibility. A high expected gain cannot compensate for a hard blocker.
7. **Rank eligible candidates** by expected verified gain, evidence confidence, reversibility and burden. Tie means unresolved preference; deterministic ordering is serialization only.
8. **Act only through real authority**. Recommendation is not authorization. Caller-declared authority is metadata, not proof.
9. **Emit a ManagementReceipt** with baseline, intervention, observed result, uncertainty, evidence refs, authority check and rollback availability.
10. **Learn**: write M+ for reproduced improvements; M- for failed proxies, interventions, hidden dependencies or unintended harms.

## Hard boundaries

```text
Presence != Leadership
HoursWorked != VerifiedOutcome
Activity != Value
Recommendation != Authority
Metric != PersonnelTruth
Correlation != Causality
SoftwarePASS != OrganizationalEffectiveness
LocalPASS != GlobalPASS
```

Do not use this skill as an automated disciplinary, hiring, firing, compensation or protected-trait inference engine. High-impact personnel decisions require appropriate human review and applicable policy/legal constraints.

## Preferred output

A compact receipt:

```text
Outcome → Evidence → Residual → Candidates → Gates → BEST/HOLD/NONE → Receipt → Follow-up observation
```

When uncertainty is material, return `HOLD` rather than manufacturing a winner.
