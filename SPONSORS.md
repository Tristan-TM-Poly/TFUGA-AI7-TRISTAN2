# Sponsoring Tristan-TM-Poly

GitHub Sponsors supports the continued development, testing, documentation, and public release of reproducible research software in this repository.

## What support funds

Current priorities include:

- OAK-safe repository and research auditing;
- reproducible scientific-computing kernels;
- tests, benchmarks, schemas, and documentation;
- privacy, IP, provenance, and negative-memory gates;
- public demonstrations that distinguish hypotheses from evidence;
- maintenance of reusable open-source components.

## What sponsorship does not buy

Sponsorship is not:

- an investment or promise of financial return;
- a purchase of intellectual property;
- a guaranteed scientific result;
- unlimited consulting or custom development;
- priority access to private, unsafe, or patent-sensitive material;
- permission to bypass privacy, security, legal, or OAK gates.

Custom services, audits, research contracts, licenses, or institutional work require a separate bounded agreement.

## Proposed sustainable tiers

These descriptions are design guidance for the GitHub Sponsors profile. Actual tiers remain subject to GitHub approval and explicit publication in the Sponsors dashboard.

### Supporter

General support for public research software and documentation. No individualized deliverable is promised.

### Research Follower

A concise public progress and roadmap digest. Benefits remain collective and bounded.

### Prototype Backer

Early visibility into selected public experimental releases when available. Experimental access is not a performance or delivery guarantee.

### Research Patron

Periodic collective technical briefings or public demonstrations, subject to capacity and safety/IP review.

### Institutional Supporter

Public recognition and a bounded exploratory conversation. Procurement, confidentiality, deliverables, or consulting require a separate agreement.

## Evidence policy

Public claims should identify their status:

- speculative;
- exploratory;
- crystallizable;
- demonstrated;
- canonical;
- archived.

Names, generated volume, stars, page views, and Sponsors approval are not evidence of scientific truth, product-market fit, income, or profitability.

## Privacy

No sponsor should submit banking data, tax identifiers, credentials, proprietary source code, confidential client data, or sensitive personal information through public issues or discussions.

Banking and tax configuration remains inside authorized GitHub, Stripe, banking, and government interfaces.

## Current first validation target

The first service-led experiment is **OAKGate Repository Audit**: a bounded assessment of an explicitly authorized repository with machine-readable findings, a human-readable report, limitations, residual risks, and prioritized next actions.

The experiment is successful only when external utility is observed. Sponsorship and paid work are never fabricated or inferred from internal activity.
