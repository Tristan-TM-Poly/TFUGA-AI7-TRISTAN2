# OAK / BOOK0 Contract — Research Civilization OS v1

## Reconstruction contract

A valid research seed must be sufficient to rebuild every materialized unit needed by the plan and preserve the irreducible scientific control roles. It must not silently promote speculative or simulation-only claims into verified durable knowledge.

## Required seed fields

- question;
- residual IDs;
- materialized unit blueprints;
- verified claim receipts (status, independent identities, provenance, tests);
- finite compilation policy;
- source plan hash;
- semantic version.

## Minimality test

Remove one seed element and replay regeneration. If required capability or verification closure remains unchanged, the element is a candidate for removal from BOOK0. If closure or auditability fails, the element is necessary.

## OAK gates

- Provenance gate: no verified claim without provenance.
- Independence gate: producer, falsifier, verifier must be distinct identities.
- Epistemic gate: output status cannot exceed evidence status.
- Reality gate: simulated evidence is not marked as observed verification.
- Complexity gate: new active structure must pay complexity rent.
- Meta-stop gate: recursion stops at finite policy depth or insufficient gain.
- Regeneration gate: missing parents/control roles cause hard failure.
- Authority gate: this kernel does not execute external side effects.

## Claim ledger rule

A future persistent claim ledger should store version, scope, provenance, evidence class, falsifiers, dependencies, verifier identity, uncertainty, and conditions for re-evaluation. v1 stores only the minimal subset needed to prove the architecture's core gates.
