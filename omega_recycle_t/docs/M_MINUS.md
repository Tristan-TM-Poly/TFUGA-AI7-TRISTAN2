## Anti-overclaim rule

A route score, optimization certificate, calibration metric, drift alarm, dataset hash, source parser, revision diff, campaign win or LCIA result each proves only its declared local contract. None independently proves sustainability, profitability, safety, regulatory compliance, causality or real-world superiority.
