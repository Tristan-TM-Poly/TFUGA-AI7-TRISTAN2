## Anti-overclaim rule

A route score, optimization certificate, independent-solver agreement, calibration metric, drift alarm, holdout result, dataset hash, live manifest, source parser, revision diff, campaign win or LCIA result proves only its declared local contract. None independently proves sustainability, profitability, safety, regulatory compliance, causality, formal correctness or real-world superiority.
