# Knowledge Rights Audit Pilot — R0.1

## Positioning

A proof-first pilot for organizations that need to share research, code, datasets, prototypes or AI context without relying on a static NDA as the entire control system.

The pilot does **not** sell legal advice or promise enforceability. It produces structured governance artifacts for review by the organization's qualified counsel, privacy/security teams and technical owners.

## Offer

### 1. Knowledge Rights Diagnostic

Inputs:
- bounded asset inventory;
- current NDA/policy samples supplied by the client;
- actor / purpose / operation matrix;
- publication, IP, privacy and AI-use constraints.

Outputs:
- KnowledgeRightsGenome map;
- conflicting/ambiguous permissions report;
- ANTI-NDA alternatives;
- minimum-disclosure opportunities;
- prioritized residuals and evidence needs.

### 2. Controlled Disclosure Pilot

Adds:
- DisclosureCapsules for selected collaboration scenarios;
- policy-as-code representation;
- DisclosureReceipt format;
- fail-closed `ALLOW | REDACT | SANDBOX | ESCALATE | DENY` workflow;
- reconstruction-risk exercises on synthetic or authorized test material.

### 3. Enterprise / Research Consortium Integration

Candidate integrations after a successful bounded pilot:
- GitHub repository/workflow permissions;
- Google Drive document/data governance;
- AI context and model-use policies;
- publication/IP gates;
- provenance and Evidence Ledger;
- dashboards for active permissions, expiry, conflicts and release candidates.

## Economic hypothesis

Customer value must be demonstrated rather than asserted. Primary measurable hypotheses:

1. fewer ambiguous access/disclosure decisions;
2. lower manual review time for repeat collaboration patterns;
3. lower accidental over-disclosure in benchmark scenarios;
4. improved provenance of what was shared, when and for what purpose;
5. faster safe collaboration compared with a document-only control workflow.

## OAK commercial gates

Do not claim:
- that the system creates legally enforceable NDAs by itself;
- that an automated classification establishes ownership or trade-secret status;
- that semantic similarity proves misuse or derivation;
- that cryptographic/zero-knowledge guarantees exist before implementation and verification;
- compliance with a jurisdiction without qualified review.

Do require before scale:
- named baseline;
- measurable customer outcome;
- evidence receipts;
- rollback/reversibility plan;
- privacy and security review;
- counsel review for legal production use.

## Pilot CTA

The strongest initial call to action is not “buy an NDA generator.” It is:

> Bring one real collaboration workflow and one bounded asset set. We map who can do what, identify ambiguity and unnecessary disclosure, then benchmark a proof-carrying policy workflow against the current process.

This keeps the first transaction falsifiable, bounded and directly connected to measurable organizational value.
