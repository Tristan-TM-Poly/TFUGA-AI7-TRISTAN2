# Ω-FLUID-T∞² — Epistemic boundary

## Established in this module

The analytic formulas, dimensionless numbers and diffusion equation implemented here are standard mathematical or physical relations under their declared assumptions.

## Computationally tested

The test suite checks exact analytic identities, numerical convergence, stability bounds, deterministic indexing, checkpointed generation and hash-chain continuity.

## Not established by this module

This module does not:

- solve the general Navier–Stokes existence and smoothness problem;
- prove a universal turbulence grammar;
- certify a new constitutive law;
- validate a medical, aerospace, pressure or energy system;
- demonstrate that FFWT outperforms standard transforms;
- turn generated combinations into physical discoveries;
- remove physical, financial, service or safety limits.

## Required anti-overclaim language

Generated frontier objects must be described as **research cells**, **candidates**, **test plans** or **hypotheses** until evidence supports stronger status.

## OAK hierarchy

- `IDEA`: named but not formalized;
- `FORMALIZED`: objects, assumptions and test path exist;
- `IMPLEMENTED`: executable representation exists;
- `TESTED`: direct tests pass;
- `BENCHMARKED`: comparison against a declared baseline exists;
- `SIMULATED`: numerical results exist under explicit assumptions;
- `MEASURED`: experimental observations exist;
- `CERTIFIED_COMPUTATIONAL`: reproducible computational claims survive declared gates;
- `CERTIFIED_PHYSICS`: measurement, uncertainty, domain of validity and independent reproduction are present;
- `REFUTED` or `ARCHIVED`: failures and reasons are preserved.

## Memory-negative rule

Every failed conservation, stability, convergence, provenance, duplication or overclaim gate should produce an M⁻ record containing observation, root cause, missing test, repair and anti-recurrence rule.
