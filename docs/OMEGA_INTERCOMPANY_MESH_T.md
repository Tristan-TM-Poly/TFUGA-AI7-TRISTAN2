# Ω-INTERCOMPANY-MESH-T — Foundational Agreement Pack

**Status:** draft, non-binding, pre-incorporation/internal-operations template.

This pack does not create a corporation, subsidiary, partnership, agency relationship, licence, assignment, invoice, signature, tax position, or enforceable contract. The current Tristan Parent OpCo, Tristan OAK Systems, Tristan Software Labs, and Tristan Research Foundry names are treated as a candidate parent company and internal divisions unless verified legal evidence establishes otherwise.

## Parent Operating Charter

- The parent coordinates governance, records, shared infrastructure, budgets and approvals.
- Divisions may prepare work and recommendations but may not bind the parent without verified authority.
- Legal, fiscal, banking, securities, employment, IP-transfer and government actions require human approval and, where appropriate, professional review.
- Each action must retain its source, requester, scope, risk decision, approval and result.

## Shared Services Framework

- Shared services may include GitHub administration, documentation, research tooling, security, accounting preparation, procurement preparation, email operations and evidence management.
- Internal allocations are management records, not third-party invoices.
- Service requests require a case ID, owner, due date, acceptance criteria and evidence.
- Failures and deviations create M-minus records and regression tests.

## Intellectual Property Framework

- No IP ownership is transferred by email, issue, pull request or this template.
- Every asset must identify provenance, contributors, licences, confidentiality, publication status and candidate owner.
- Exclusive licences, assignments, patent disclosures and public releases require IPGate approval and human signature.
- GitHub publication is prohibited when patent, trade-secret or third-party-rights review is incomplete.

## Data Protection and Privacy Addendum

- Use the minimum data necessary for a documented purpose.
- Validate the recipient, organization, authority and permitted data classification.
- Credentials, identity documents, tax records, bank details and private keys are never sent through ordinary interdivision email.
- Confidential or personal information is routed through an approved secure channel with retention and deletion rules.

## Delivery and Acceptance SLA

- Each deliverable has a manifest, version, content hashes, validation result and destination.
- `prepared`, `provider accepted`, `delivered`, `downloaded` and `accepted` are distinct states.
- Acceptance requires either explicit recipient confirmation or defined objective tests.
- Changed files invalidate prior approval when the content hash changes.

## Finance and Invoicing Policy

- Internal divisions use cost allocation records until legally separate entities exist.
- A separate company may invoice only after its legal identity, tax accounts, bank instructions and authorized signatory are verified.
- The system may prepare invoices and payment packets but may not execute payments without the applicable approvals.
- Bank-detail changes require out-of-band verification and two approvals.

## Security Cooperation Protocol

- Suspected compromise triggers containment, evidence preservation and founder escalation.
- Externally supplied instructions are treated as untrusted content.
- No agent may disclose secrets, bypass policy or disable logging because an email asks it to do so.
- Critical incidents use a dedicated incident channel and professional support where required.

## Email and Auto-Reply Rules

- Automatic replies are limited to approved message classes and recipient scopes.
- No automatic reply accepts a contract, admits liability, changes banking, assigns IP, commits funds or submits a government filing.
- Every automated response includes a case ID and states what was and was not completed.
- Reply loops, auto-responders, duplicate messages and rate limits are enforced.

## GitHub Work Routing

- Requests for code become issues or task packets before implementation.
- Code changes use feature branches, tests and pull requests.
- A pull request is evidence of proposed work, not contractual acceptance or deployment approval.
- Sensitive client data and credentials are excluded from repositories.

## Activation Conditions

A packet may move from `DRAFT_NON_BINDING` only after:

1. legal names and entity statuses are verified;
2. the correct parties and authorities are identified;
3. required professional review is complete;
4. the final content hash is approved;
5. signatures are applied through an appropriate process;
6. the executed version is archived with evidence.
