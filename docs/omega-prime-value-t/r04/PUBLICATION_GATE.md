# R0.4 publication gate

A candidate may move from internal evidence to a public mathematical claim only after all applicable gates pass.

## Mathematical gate

- deterministic certificate verifies;
- recursive dependencies verify;
- a second implementation reproduces the result;
- all artifact hashes and exact commands are retained.

## Precedence gate

- authoritative databases are checked at pinned dates;
- query scope and coverage limitations are recorded;
- absence is stated only within those snapshots;
- “new”, “largest” and “record” remain prohibited until reviewed by the relevant registry or community.

## Operational gate

- transparency checkpoint is created;
- signing-key identity and custody are documented;
- software versions, compiler versions and hardware receipts are retained;
- cost, energy and runtime estimates include uncertainty.

## Commercial gate

- the integer itself is not presented as exclusive property;
- the offer identifies the actual asset: implementation, certification, benchmarking, integration, support or sponsored computation;
- no return, price or customer-demand guarantee is inferred from PrimeMarketScore;
- legal and IP review precedes any patent or exclusivity language.

## Human approval

R0.4 produces reviewable evidence. It does not autonomously publish a novelty claim, submit to a record table, transfer signing keys, spend money or execute an external campaign without an explicit human decision.
