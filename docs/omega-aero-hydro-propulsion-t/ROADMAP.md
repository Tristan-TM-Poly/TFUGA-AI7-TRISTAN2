# Ω-AERO-HYDRO-PROPULSION-T∞ roadmap

## R0.1 — Rotor truth scaffold

Typed models, low-order sectional forces, uniform induction, tip loss, cavitation screen, multiobjective grid, OAK, tests and CI.

## R0.2 — Validated BEM

Annular induction, tangential induction, high-thrust corrections, hub/tip losses, polar interpolation, Reynolds/Mach tables and reference-case comparisons.

## R0.3 — Airfoil and hydrofoil atlas

NACA import, coordinate normalization, measured-polar provenance, uncertainty bands, stall hysteresis and roughness sensitivity.

## R0.4 — Wake and unsteady rotor

Prescribed/free wake, azimuthal discretization, rotor–stator interaction, gusts, dynamic inflow and blade-passing spectra.

## R0.5 — Compressible propulsion components

Quasi-1D nozzles, inlets, diffusers, compressor/turbine maps, choking, shocks and cycle coupling.

## R0.6 — Hydro-propulsion

Cavitation inception maps, ventilation, free surface, erosion risk, hull/propulsor interaction and water-jet components.

## R0.7 — FSI and durability

Beam/shell blade models, centrifugal stress, modal analysis, Campbell diagrams, flutter, fatigue and uncertainty.

## R0.8 — Thermal and combustion bridge

Brayton-cycle objects, combustor mixing, heat transfer, cooling passages, material temperature limits and emissions ledgers.

## R0.9 — Robust inverse design

Adjoints, surrogate models, Bayesian optimization, manufacturing constraints, off-design envelopes and Pareto robustness.

## R1.0 — PropulsionOAKBench

Portable evidence bundles comparing low-, medium- and high-fidelity predictions against traceable experimental references.
