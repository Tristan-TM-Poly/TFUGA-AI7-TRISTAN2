## Multi-type ontology

The atlas distinguishes theories, kernels, engines, laboratories, forges,
operating systems, governance systems, evidence systems, campaigns, datasets,
products, services, ventures, memories and repositories.

A record is a pointer. It does not prove that the referenced branch is merged,
integrated, alive, externally validated, used or valuable.
