# Architecture

## Four planes

1. **Evidence plane** — sources, licences, uncertainty, hashes and status.
2. **Mirror plane** — observed/public, synthetic physical, causal, organizational and counterfactual projections.
3. **Experiment plane** — worlds, interventions, flow, outage, restoration, CVCD and Pareto comparison.
4. **Governance plane** — constitutional chambers, safety gates, claim boundaries and human sovereignty.

## Core equation

\[
\mathbb H_{QC}(t)=H_{energy}\otimes H_{hydro}\otimes H_{climate}\otimes H_{assets}\otimes H_{organization}\otimes H_{territory}\otimes H_{evidence}.
\]

R0.1 materializes only a safe regional synthetic projection. Finer fidelity is not implied.

## Separation of powers

```text
propose != verify != authorize != execute
```

The package implements proposal and verification. Authorization remains human. Live execution is absent.

## Multi-model discipline

A future R0.2 should compare DC flow, AC benchmark fixtures, graph surrogates and causal models. Agreement increases robustness; disagreement becomes a first-class OAK residue.
