# Roadmap

## R0.1 — public synthetic kernel

Complete in this PR: mirror, flow, hazards, campaigns, parliament, OAKBench and security.

## R0.2 — evidence ingestion

- source adapters restricted to documented open endpoints;
- licence and update-cadence registry;
- temporal versioning and claim-evidence graph;
- no hidden crawling or access-control bypass.

## R0.3 — model pluralism

- additional solver baselines;
- calibration on openly publishable aggregates;
- uncertainty propagation and sensitivity analysis;
- FFWT/SVD/DMD comparisons with explicit negative results.

## R0.4 — research platform

- OAKGridBench challenge fixtures;
- reproducible notebooks and generated reports;
- partner workspaces with strict data separation;
- public citizen twin using only safe aggregation.

## R1.0 gate

Requires independent review, threat modelling, provenance audit, reproducible benchmarks, legal review, and an explicit decision on whether the project remains public-only or obtains an official authorized partner environment.
