## Meta-generalization

The primitive is a verified transformation candidate, not a theory name. A transformation may affect theory, code, experiment, simulation, document, publication, product or workflow, while preserving their semantic distinctions.

Shared evaluation axes include verified value, evidence, reuse, reachability, regenerability, interoperability, transferability, cost, proof debt, semantic debt, uncertainty, irreversibility, fragility and risk.

