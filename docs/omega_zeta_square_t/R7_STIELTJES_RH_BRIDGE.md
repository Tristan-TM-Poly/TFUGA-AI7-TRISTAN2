# R7 — All-orders Stieltjes/Hankel bridge to RH

Status: `DERIVED_EQUIVALENCE_CANDIDATE`, `DEPENDENCIES_IDENTIFIED`, `NOT_YET_PROMOTED_TO_PROVED`.

This note isolates a potentially much stronger route than explicit tail-by-tail
finite truncation. The point is to use the **all-orders** Stieltjes moment theorem
and analytic continuation of the logarithmic derivative.

## 1. Canonical reciprocal-zero moments

Let

\[
\Theta(u)=\xi\!\left(\frac12+\sqrt u\right),
\]

whose zeros are the centered-square zero images \(u_n\). Since
\(\Theta(0)=\xi(1/2)\ne0\), define

\[
\lambda_n=-\frac1{u_n}.
\]

Because \(\Theta\) has order \(1/2<1\), the intended genus-zero product/log
 derivative representation is

\[
\frac{\Theta(u)}{\Theta(0)}
=\prod_n(1+\lambda_nu),
\qquad
L(u):=\frac{\Theta'(u)}{\Theta(u)}
=\sum_n\frac{\lambda_n}{1+\lambda_nu}.
\]

Around \(u=0\), write

\[
L(u)=\sum_{k=0}^\infty(-1)^km_ku^k,
\qquad
m_k=\sum_n\lambda_n^{k+1}.
\]

The absolute convergence and the precise Hadamard/product justification are
explicit proof obligations in this R7 draft; they must not be replaced by a
formal manipulation.

## 2. RH implies a Stieltjes moment sequence

Under RH,

\[
u_n=-\gamma_n^2,
\qquad
\lambda_n=\gamma_n^{-2}>0.
\]

Hence

\[
m_k=\sum_n\lambda_n^{k+1}
=\int_0^\infty t^k\,d\mu(t)
\]

for the positive discrete measure

\[
\mu=\sum_n\lambda_n\,\delta_{\lambda_n}.
\]

Therefore every basic and shifted Hankel matrix generated by \((m_k)\) is
positive semidefinite. This direction is structurally straightforward once the
zero-product/moment sums are justified.

## 3. Candidate converse

Assume instead that the complete real sequence \((m_k)_{k\ge0}\) satisfies the
all-orders Stieltjes conditions:

\[
H_N^{(0)}=(m_{i+j})_{i,j=0}^{N-1}\succeq0,
\qquad
H_N^{(1)}=(m_{i+j+1})_{i,j=0}^{N-1}\succeq0
\]

for every \(N\).

By the classical Stieltjes moment theorem, there exists a positive measure
\(\mu\) on \([0,\infty)\) with moments \(m_k\).

### Exponential moment growth should force compact support

Because \(L\) is analytic in a nonzero disk around \(0\), its Taylor
coefficients have an exponential bound

\[
0\le m_k\le C R^{-k}
\]

for some \(C,R>0\), assuming the Stieltjes positivity has already supplied
\(m_k\ge0\).

For a positive measure, finite \(\limsup m_k^{1/k}\) forces bounded support:
if \(\mu([A,\infty))>0\), then

\[
m_k\ge A^k\mu([A,\infty)),
\]

so \(\limsup m_k^{1/k}\ge A\). Arbitrarily large support would contradict the
finite exponential growth bound.

Thus the representing measure should be compactly supported in some
\([0,M]\).

## 4. Stieltjes transform versus logarithmic derivative

For compactly supported positive \(\mu\), define

\[
F(u)=\int_{[0,M]}\frac{d\mu(t)}{1+tu}.
\]

Near the origin,

\[
F(u)=\sum_{k=0}^\infty(-1)^km_ku^k=L(u).
\]

The function \(F\) is holomorphic away from its negative-real singular set.
Therefore, by analytic continuation, a genuine non-real pole of \(L\) would be
incompatible with equality to \(F\) continued from a neighborhood of zero.
Likewise a positive-real pole is incompatible with a Stieltjes transform of a
positive measure on \([0,M]\).

The poles of \(L=\Theta'/\Theta\) are exactly the zeros of \(\Theta\), counted
with positive integer residues, provided there is no hidden cancellation.
Thus the intended conclusion is

\[
Z(\Theta)\subset\mathbb R_-.
\]

Using the known criterion for \(\Theta\), this is RH.

## 5. Candidate all-orders criterion

The resulting target equivalence is

\[
\boxed{
RH
\stackrel{?}{\Longleftrightarrow}
H_N^{(0)}\succeq0\text{ and }H_N^{(1)}\succeq0
\quad\text{for every }N.
}
\]

If all steps above are fully discharged, its contrapositive gives an especially
useful existential result:

\[
\boxed{
RH\text{ false}
\Longrightarrow
\exists N<\infty\text{ for which a basic or shifted Hankel matrix is not PSD}.
}
\]

That would establish existence of a finite negative certificate without an
explicit universal bound on the detection order.

## 6. Why R5/R6 still matter

R5 and R6 are finite exact models. They show concretely how a non-real spectral
pair creates indefiniteness and how finite positive background atoms can delay
its appearance. R7 would explain the infinite case at the level of moment
existence and analytic structure rather than by estimating every tail atom.

The finite theorems therefore become local witnesses and adversarial test beds
for the global criterion.

## 7. OAK proof obligations before promotion

R7 remains unpromoted until all of the following are checked independently:

1. rigorous genus-zero Hadamard product for \(\Theta\) in the exact convention used here;
2. absolute convergence of \(\sum_n\lambda_n^{k+1}\), especially the first moment;
3. correct multiplicities after quotienting \(w\leftrightarrow-w\);
4. reality of every \(m_k\) without assuming RH;
5. exact indexing between Taylor coefficients and basic/shifted Hankel matrices;
6. application of the full Stieltjes moment existence theorem, not merely leading determinants;
7. compact-support deduction from coefficient growth;
8. domain/connectivity details in the analytic-continuation argument;
9. proof that poles of \(\Theta'/\Theta\) cannot be canceled or hidden;
10. independent bibliographic search for an existing equivalent criterion;
11. formal or expert verification of both logical directions.

Until these are discharged, R7 is a high-value proof program, not a proof of RH.
