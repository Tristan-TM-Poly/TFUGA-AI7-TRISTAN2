## 6. Portfolio law

The default portfolio has a finite planning budget, a maximum cardinality and category-diversity constraints. Selection maximizes closure, evidence, reuse, product value, information value and reversibility while penalizing integration cost, risk and uncertainty.

The scalar is only a scheduling heuristic. The output preserves selected, deferred and blocked candidates, costs, covered domains and rationale.

## 7. Evidence lifecycle

Evidence is content-addressed and classified as:

- `CURRENT`;
- `STALE` after dependency, environment or test-contract drift;
- `EXPIRED` after its validity horizon;
- `SUPERSEDED` by named replacement evidence;
- `INVALIDATED` after subject or contract change;
- `REVOKED` by explicit human decision.

Promotion requires current named evidence and structured claim coverage. Hash integrity proves content identity, not correctness, causality, scientific truth or legal admissibility.
