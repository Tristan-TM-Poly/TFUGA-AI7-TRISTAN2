# Ω-METATIME-TRISTAN-T∞ — R0.1

Status: **experimental architecture + executable research prototype**.

## Purpose

Ω-METATIME treats time as a constrained transformation budget rather than a productivity score. The objective is to maximize verified, retained, transferable, reusable and regenerable capability while charging attention, compute, complexity, risk and epistemic debt.

\[
\Omega_T =
\frac{\Delta VC+\Delta Retention+\Delta Transfer+\Delta Reuse+\Delta Regeneration+\Delta OptionValue}
{Time(1+Attention+Compute+Complexity+Risk+EpistemicDebt)}
\]

High chat or idea volume is not evidence of learning or scientific progress.

## R0.1 components

- `TemporalCounters`: activity density, Verified Capability Velocity, Proof Bandwidth.
- `CapabilityDelta`: debt-adjusted temporal capability density.
- `TemporalState` + `TemporalRegime`: EXPLORE / VERIFY / BUILD / ATTACK / COMPRESS / HARVEST / RESTORE.
- `StrategyGenome`: tournament candidate with `Generator != Judge`.
- `Branching Budget`: refuses new branches when proof bandwidth is too low or branch capacity is saturated.
- `Automation Gate`: automation must eliminate more future work than it creates in build, maintenance, risk and complexity.
- `Meta Stop Rule`: META^(n+1) is forbidden when the current kernel can already express the capability, or when out-of-sample gain does not pay complexity/risk/debt.
- `TemporalCrystal`: bounded daily/periodic crystallization with SHA-256 receipt.
- `Regeneration Closure`: measures how much of the required kernel can be rebuilt.
- `Minimum Generator Cover`: greedy, explicitly heuristic distillation of generators.
- `StudentBaselineTwin`: learning speedup is reportable only when retention, transfer and calibration are non-inferior to the baseline.

## Canonical loop

GOAL → RESIDUALIZE → CHOOSE REGIME → GENERATE STRATEGIES → VERIFY → ACT/BUILD → ATTACK → CRYSTALLIZE → REGENERATE → PRUNE/MERGE → UPDATE.

## Constitution

1. Generated ≠ Verified.
2. Activity ≠ Capability.
3. Speed ≠ Learning.
4. Generator ≠ Judge.
5. Simulation ≠ Observation.
6. Reconstruction ≠ Proof.
7. More branches ≠ More progress.
8. More automation ≠ More authority.
9. Benchmark gain ≠ real-world superiority.
10. A new meta-level must pay its complexity rent.
11. Claims comparing Tristan with students or professionals require a matched baseline and measured retention/transfer/calibration.
12. The engine recommends temporal regimes; it does not autonomously publish, spend, govern, contact third parties, or execute sensitive external actions.

## Falsification targets

R0.1 should be rejected or simplified if a simpler baseline matches it on:
- verified capability per hour,
- 7/30-day retention,
- transfer,
- calibration,
- future work eliminated,
- complexity debt,
- regeneration closure.

The first meaningful benchmark is a frozen learning/build task completed both with a conventional workflow and Ω-METATIME under equal outcome criteria.
