# Ω-MANAGEMENT-T∞ R0.2 — Event-driven capability management

R0.2 extends R0.1 with a governed artifact ladder and a residual/event model.

## Core transition
`ObservedState -> ResidualField -> CandidateInterventions -> HardGates -> RankedRecommendations -> Human/Policy Authority -> Action -> Receipt -> Re-observation`

## Management objective
Minimize unnecessary intervention while preserving verified outcomes, capability growth, resilience, safety and accountability.

## Event triggers
Examples include blockage age, decision uncertainty, workload imbalance, opportunity value, reliability degradation and repeated residuals. Thresholds are policy inputs, not universal truths.

## Proxy discipline
Visible hours, meeting count, message count and office presence are weak proxies unless validated against direct outcomes in the relevant context.

## Personnel boundary
No metric in this package is a lawful or ethical substitute for contextual human review in hiring, firing, discipline, promotion, compensation or protected-trait decisions.

## 2^n ladder application
R0.1 established the kernel. R0.2 targets 16 artifact classes through materialized and regenerable surfaces. The `n+1` probe is represented explicitly and must report marginal verified gain rather than assuming more material is better.

## OAK laws
`Event != Cause`
`ThresholdCrossed != PermissionToAct`
`Recommendation != Authority`
`ObservedGain != CausalProof`
`ProxyCorrelation != LeadershipTruth`
`MoreArtifacts != MoreValue`
