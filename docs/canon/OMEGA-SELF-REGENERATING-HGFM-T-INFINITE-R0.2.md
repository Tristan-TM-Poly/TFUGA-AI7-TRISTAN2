## New primitives

- `CausalHyperedge`: intervention/outcome/confounder/counterfactual metadata.
- `ExperimentHyperedge`: one experiment may update many claims.

- `CounterfactualWorld`: competing intervention worlds.
- M⁻ `scope_pattern`: prune known-bad structural families before insertion.
- `best_experiment()`: maximize information gain × affected claims / (cost+risk).

## Core invariants

- causal claim != association claim;
- experiment count != claim count;
- fractal analogy != mathematical renormalization theorem;
- a virtual/candidate edge != evidence;
- M⁻ pruning remains reviewable and reversible;
- regeneration score measures recovered verified capabilities, not identity of graph structure.
